(01) The idea

Your phone already has a better camera than your webcam. OpenStream sends it straight into OBS over your own Wi-Fi — no capture card, no screen mirroring, no account, no cloud.

(02) How it works

FOUR HOPS.
ONE NETWORK.

MediaSRT :9100DiscoveryUDP :51615Camera controlHTTP :9101
01capture

Camera2

The app opens your phone's real camera hardware — every lens it exposes, plus the front camera and microphone.

02encode

Hardware encode

The phone's own encoder compresses to H.264 video and AAC audio. Your computer does zero encoding work.

03transmit

SRT over Wi-Fi

Packed into MPEG-TS and sent over SRT — a protocol built for live video on imperfect networks.

04receive

Native OBS source

The plugin decodes straight into an OBS source that behaves like any webcam in your scenes.

(06) Go live

FIVE MOVES.
THEN YOU'RE ON.

1

Same network

Phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi. 5 GHz or Wi-Fi 6 if you have it.

2

Add the source

In OBS: Sources → + → OpenStream. One source per phone.

3

Pick your computer

Open the app — your PC appears under “Choose your OBS computer”. Tap it.

4

Frame the shot

Choose a lens and quality. Turn on Stay to keep the screen awake.

5

Go live

Stream or record as usual. Fine-tune from the Camera Control dock.
