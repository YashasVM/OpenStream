## Video direction

v3 (premium minimal): monochrome. Logo-black (#0B0B0C) for the quiet open and the close, warm paper
(#F3EFE8) for the product section, one ink colour, warm-grey secondary text. Inter 500–700 with tight
tracking. Starts low (soft pads, slow fades, crossfades) and builds to the drop at 16s, where the
picture switches from dark to light. Every frame is a CSS-3D stage under a perspective camera with slow,
smooth moves; objects with real depth (3D phone, 3D app tile, tilted spec table). The logo is the real
Android launcher mark rebuilt as a vector (it draws itself in frames 4 and 10). Frames are generated by
tools/build_frames_v3.py.
