[span] (02) How it works
[h2] FOUR HOPS.
[span] ONE NETWORK.
[b] Media
[span] SRT :9100
[b] Discovery
[span] UDP :51615
[b] Camera control
[span] HTTP :9101
[span] capture
[h3] Camera2
[p] The app opens your phone's real camera hardware — every lens it exposes, plus the front camera and microphone.
[span] encode
[h3] Hardware encode
[p] The phone's own encoder compresses to H.264 video and AAC audio. Your computer does zero encoding work.
[span] transmit
[h3] SRT over Wi-Fi
[p] Packed into MPEG-TS and sent over SRT — a protocol built for live video on imperfect networks.
[span] receive
[h3] Native OBS source
[p] The plugin decodes straight into an OBS source that behaves like any webcam in your scenes.
[span] (03) Features
[h3] Every lens
[p] Ultra-wide, main, telephoto and front. Switch on the phone or from OBS.
[h3] Control from OBS
[p] A Camera Control dock inside OBS for lens, zoom and torch. Hands off the phone.
[h3] Finds your PC
[p] OBS computers on your network appear in the app. Tap one. No IP addresses.
[h3] Reconnects itself
[p] Brief Wi-Fi drops recover on their own. The source stays put in your scene.
[h3] Real audio
[p] AAC audio from the phone mic, synced with the video and on its own track.
[h3] Local only
[p] No accounts, no relay, no telemetry. Video never leaves your network.

[span] (06) Go live
[h2] FIVE MOVES.
[span] THEN YOU'RE ON.
[h4] Same network
[p] Phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi. 5 GHz or Wi-Fi 6 if you have it.
[h4] Add the source
[p] In OBS: Sources → + → OpenStream. One source per phone.
[h4] Pick your computer
[p] Open the app — your PC appears under “Choose your OBS computer”. Tap it.
[h4] Frame the shot
[p] Choose a lens and quality. Turn on Stay to keep the screen awake.
[h4] Go live
[p] Stream or record as usual. Fine-tune from the Camera Control dock.
